# Code of Conduct

PAICHAI NEXUS is an interdisciplinary student project organization.

Members and contributors are expected to:

- communicate professionally
- critique ideas and evidence rather than individuals
- respect differences in academic background and experience
- document decisions that affect collaborators
- avoid misrepresenting unverified work as validated results
- respect privacy, research ethics, and institutional requirements
- make room for domain experts to challenge technical assumptions
- make room for technical contributors to challenge unsupported claims

Project leadership may limit participation when behavior repeatedly harms collaboration, safety, research integrity, or other members.
