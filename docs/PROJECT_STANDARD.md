# NEXUS Project Standard

This document defines the minimum structure expected from PAICHAI NEXUS projects.

## 1. Problem

What problem is being solved?

Avoid solution-first descriptions.

## 2. Background

Why does the problem matter?

Include domain context and known constraints.

## 3. Objectives

Define concrete project or research objectives.

## 4. Stakeholders

Who uses, evaluates, operates, or is affected by the result?

## 5. Requirements / Research Questions

Separate:

- confirmed requirements
- assumptions
- hypotheses
- unresolved questions

## 6. Architecture / Research Design

Describe the system boundary or experimental design.

## 7. Team & Responsibilities

Document ownership by function and domain.

## 8. Milestones

Use measurable checkpoints.

## 9. Validation Method

Define what evidence would show that the system, model, experiment, or service works.

## 10. Evidence

Examples:

- test results
- experiment logs
- performance metrics
- screenshots
- user-study results
- expert review
- field-test results

## 11. Current Status

Use explicit maturity language:

```text
Concept
Implemented
Demonstrated
Validated
Field Verified
```

## 12. Limitations

State known weaknesses, missing tests, and unresolved assumptions.

## 13. Next Steps

Every project should have an explicit continuation path.
